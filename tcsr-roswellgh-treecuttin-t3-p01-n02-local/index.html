Sub-Grade Stump Grinding & Root Flare Remediation in Roswell, GA

Felling a tree is only half the job. Unsightly stumps left behind attract destructive termites, carpenter ants, and root rot fungi while creating lawnmower trip hazards. Sub-grade mechanical stump grinding clears the root flare and restores your yard for turf re-seeding or replanting in Roswell.

Mechanical Stump Grinding to 6-12 Inches Below Grade

Commercial stump grinders utilize high-speed carbide-tipped cutting wheels to pulverize hardwood stumps into nutrient-rich organic wood chips. Grinding reaches 6 to 12 inches below ground level to eliminate the primary root crown and prevent unwanted sucker regrowth.

  • Deep sub-grade grinding eliminating the core trunk plate and lateral root flares
  • Protective debris shielding preventing flying wood chips from striking windows or cars
  • Eradicating termite nesting habitat and carpenter ant colonies near residential homes

Chasing Surface Roots & Backfilling the Stump Cavity

Large surface roots snaking through turf are chased and ground down to restore level grade. Once grinding finishes, wood chips can be cleared away or backfilled into the hole mixed with nitrogen topsoil to settle before laying sod.

  • Grinding exposed surface lateral roots radiating across lawn and garden beds
  • Option to backfill stump cavities with blended topsoil ready for fresh sod installation
  • Repurposing pulverized hardwood woodchips as weed-suppressing landscape bed mulch
  • Checking for buried utility lines and sprinkler pipes prior to grinding engagement
