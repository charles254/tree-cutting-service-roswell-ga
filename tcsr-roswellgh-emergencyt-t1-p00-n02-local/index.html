Immediate Site Hazards: Downed Lines, Root Plate Tension & Crown Hangars

Immediately following a storm, safety is the first priority. Never approach electrical wires intertwined with fallen timber, as damp wood conducts high-voltage electricity. Uprooted root balls hold immense kinetic tension that can snap violently when weight is removed.

  • Maintain a minimum 30-foot clearance from downed wires and notify Georgia Power immediately
  • Keep children and pets away from suspended widow-maker limbs lodged in canopies
  • Inspect crawlspaces and foundations adjacent to uprooted root plates for structural shift
  • Establish a secure exterior boundary perimeter until professional crews arrive

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can your emergency tree crew respond in Roswell GA?

Our emergency dispatch operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For trees fallen on homes, structures, or driveways, crews are mobilized promptly upon emergency dispatch call.

Does homeowners insurance cover tree removal after a storm?

Homeowners insurance typically covers tree removal costs if a fallen tree has caused physical impact damage to an insured structure such as a roof, garage, or fence. We provide detailed photographic evidence and itemized invoices for adjusters.

What should I do if a tree branch falls on power lines near my home?

Stay clear by at least 30 feet and contact Georgia Power immediately. Never attempt to prune or touch branches near power lines yourself, as wet wood conducts lethal electric voltage.
