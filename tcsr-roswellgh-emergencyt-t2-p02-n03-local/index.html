Frequently asked questions

What commercial liability insurance should tree contractors maintain?

Commercial tree contractors should carry at least $2,000,000 in commercial general liability insurance and statutory workers' compensation coverage for all ground and aerial staff.

Can tree work be scheduled during non-business hours for commercial sites?

Yes. Commercial tree teams can schedule evening or weekend operations to prevent disruptions to retail customer parking, office operations, or traffic flow.

Checklist

  • Conduct a 360-degree visual inspection for trunk decay, dead canopy limbs, or root heaving.
  • Check clearance distances from overhead service lines, roof shingles, and neighboring fences.
  • Verify that your tree service provider carries active general liability and worker compensation certificates.
  • Confirm whether a City of Roswell tree removal permit or arborist report is required.
  • Ensure the written estimate includes complete branch chipping, wood disposal, and ground raking.
