Overhead Utility Clearance & Power Line Tree Pruning in Roswell, GA

Overhanging tree limbs contacting overhead electrical lines cause sudden power outages, transformer blowouts, and hazardous electrical fire risks. Trimming near high-voltage lines requires specialized insulated equipment, certified line-clearance arborists, and strict safety compliance in Roswell.

High-Voltage Electrical Hazards & Safe Distance Thresholds

Trees growing near power lines must never be pruned by non-certified workers. Damp timber conducts electricity, creating lethal ground-fault arcs. Certified utility tree workers utilize fiberglass insulated poles and dielectric bucket booms to clear limbs safely.

  • Maintaining OSHA-mandated Minimum Approach Distances (MAD) from energized conductors
  • Using fiberglass-insulated poles and non-conductive synthetic rigging ropes
  • Never attempting to cut limbs touching primary distribution power lines yourself
  • Immediate Georgia Power utility coordination when tree limbs rest on live power lines

Service Drop Clearance for Residential Roswell Homes

While power utilities maintain main street distribution lines, homeowners are typically responsible for keeping tree limbs clear of the service drop line running from the street pole directly to their house weatherhead.

  • Trimming branches to maintain safe 5-to-10-foot buffers from residential service drops
  • Preventing heavy snow, ice, or wind-loaded limbs from ripping service masts off homes
  • Coordinating temporary utility service disconnects for complex tree removal near service drops
  • Directional pruning encouraging future branch growth away from overhead utility lines
