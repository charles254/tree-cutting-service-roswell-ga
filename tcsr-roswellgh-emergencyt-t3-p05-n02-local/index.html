Documenting Storm Impact Damage for Quick Claim Processing

Before any tree cutting begins, thorough photographic and video documentation is critical. Photograph wide-angle perspective shots showing the entire fallen tree and close-up images of crushed roof trusses, gutters, shattered windows, or damaged fencing.

Understanding Policy Coverage: Structure Clearance vs Debris Hauling

Standard homeowners insurance policies cover tree extraction off an insured structure (house, garage, shed). However, policies often feature specific sub-limits (e.g., $500 to $1,000) for hauling away tree wood that fell harmlessly in open yard areas.

  • Full coverage typically applies to extracting trees resting directly on insured structures
  • Emergency structural tarping costs are generally covered under temporary repairs
  • Working directly with insurance adjusters to provide required itemized labor estimates
  • Reviewing deductible amounts and policy sub-limits for open-yard debris removal
