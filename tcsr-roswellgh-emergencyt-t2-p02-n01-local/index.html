Understanding City of Roswell Chapter 21 Tree Preservation Regulations

The City of Roswell enforces strict tree canopy conservation policies designed to preserve local tree cover. Removing protected specimen species without an approved city permit can result in substantial stop-work orders, civil fines per caliper inch, and mandatory mitigation plantings.

  • Municipal definition of specimen trees based on species and diameter at breast height (DBH)
  • Hardwoods (Oaks, Hickories, Poplars) typically qualifying at 24 inches DBH or greater
  • Southern Pines qualifying as specimen trees at 30 inches DBH or greater
  • Understory trees (Dogwoods, Redbuds) qualifying as specimen trees at 10 inches DBH

Permit Application Requirements & Hazardous Tree Exemptions

For healthy specimen trees, property owners must submit an official tree removal permit application along with site plans and arboricultural justification. Dead, severely diseased, or structurally hazardous trees presenting imminent risk qualify for expedited reviews or emergency exemptions with arborist photo documentation.

  • Submitting written tree condition documentation from an ISA Certified Arborist
  • Clear photographic records showing structural cavities, severe lean, or trunk rot
  • Emergency tree removal exemptions when storm damage threatens life-safety or structures
  • Adhering to required replacement tree replanting or tree bank recompense payments

Frequently asked questions

How long does tree permit processing take in Roswell GA?

Standard municipal tree permit reviews by the City of Roswell Community Development Department typically take 5 to 10 business days. Hazardous emergency trees receive expedited reviews.

Does Homeowners Association (HOA) approval replace a City of Roswell tree permit?

No. HOA approval does not supersede municipal law. Property owners must obtain required City of Roswell tree permits regardless of HOA clearance before cutting specimen trees.
